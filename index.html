Volvo Home Assistant Integration – Terms of Use

This application is a private personal integration used solely by the application owner to connect their Volvo vehicle with a private Home Assistant installation.

Vehicle data is used only for personal home automation, energy management, charging monitoring, vehicle status and related functions.
